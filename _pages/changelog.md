# Changelog

<br>

## iOS

<br>

### `Latest`
### **Version 6.5**
Jul 12, 2026
#### What's New
- Account creation and sign-in are now available to everyone, even before purchasing a subscription (premium features like Cloud Sync still require a subscription)

<br>

### **Version 6.4**
Jun 12, 2026
#### What's New
- Fixed a crash on launch that affected some users who had updated from an older version
- You are now notified if your data could not be recovered after an update
- Renamed "Night Mode" to "Dark Mode" in Settings
- Added crash reporting to help us identify and fix issues more quickly

<br>

### **Version 6.3**
May 31, 2026
#### What's New
- Simplified the text selection menu by removing the redundant "Copy (no ruby)" option — use Hide Furigana in Settings to copy text without ruby
- Hyperlink color in content views is now easier to read in dark mode
- Improved app startup performance

<br>

### **Version 6.2**
May 23, 2026
#### What's New
- Added Hide Furigana setting to hide ruby annotations for a cleaner reading experience (Gappon Premium)
- Fixed a crash when adding a highlight with cloud sync enabled
- Fixed content background not extending to the bottom of the screen

<br>

### **Version 6.1**
May 19, 2026
#### What's New
- Fixed sync not updating when reopening the app after changes were made on another device
- Fixed highlight marks occasionally remaining in scripture text after cross-device deletion
- Fixed a crash when tapping a highlight deleted on another device

<br>

# **Version 6.0**
May 10, 2026
#### What's New
- UI polish across the app for a clearer, more consistent experience.
- Faster, more reliable startup when you launch the app.
- Copy selected text without furigana (ruby)—only for your selection, not forced full-verse copy.
- Pricing is now Gappon Premium (subscription) instead of the one-time All Features Pass. Existing pass purchases remain valid.
